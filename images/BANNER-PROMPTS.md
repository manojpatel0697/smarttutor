# Banner images for the top slider

Save each image in this folder with the exact file name below. The website picks it up automatically and replaces the drawn illustration on that slide.

- Size: **1920 × 720 px** (wide banner, 8:3). Landscape JPG, under ~400 KB.
- Keep the **left 45%** of the image calm and darker. The headline text sits there.
- Put the main subject (students and teacher) on the **right side**.

Paste the prompts into any AI image tool (Gemini / Imagen, Midjourney, DALL·E, Firefly, Ideogram).

---

## banner-1.jpg — "Admissions Open"

> Wide cinematic photo of a bright, modern tuition classroom in Bengaluru, India. A small group of Indian school students aged 12–15 in casual school uniforms are sitting at wooden desks with notebooks and textbooks, smiling and attentive. A friendly Indian woman teacher stands at a whiteboard on the right side of the frame. Warm natural daylight, soft depth of field. Navy blue and mustard yellow accents in the room. The left third of the image is softly blurred, empty wall space. Realistic photography, 35mm, natural skin tones, no text, no logos. Aspect ratio 8:3.

Subjects placed on the right side of the frame, the left side calm and out of focus. Natural, candid, documentary-style photography, no text, no logos. Aspect ratio 8:3.

### Tips
- If the tool can't make 8:3, make **16:9** and crop to 1920 × 720.
- Check hands, faces and any writing in the image for AI mistakes before you use it.
- Real photos of the centre and its students (with parents' permission) always look better than AI images. Swap them in when the client sends some.
- Optional extra photos: `classroom.jpg` (About section, 4:3) is used the same way.
